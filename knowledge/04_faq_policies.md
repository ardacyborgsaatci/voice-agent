# Frequently asked questions and policies

## Orders and shipping
- We ship across the country. Standard shipping is 5 dollars and takes 3 to 5 business days. Orders over 60 dollars ship free.
- Coffee is roasted within 48 hours of shipping, so every bag arrives fresh.
- We ship to addresses in the US only.

## Returns and refunds
- If your coffee arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, email us within 14 days with a photo. We'll send a replacement or refund you.
- We don't accept returns on opened bags for taste reasons, but we'll happily suggest a different blend.
- Gift cards are non-refundable.

## Subscriptions
- Subscribe and save 10 percent on any bag. Choose delivery every 2, 4, or 6 weeks.
- You can skip, pause, or cancel a subscription at any time from your account page, or by calling us.

## Allergies and dietary questions
- All our coffee is produced in a facility that also handles tree nuts. Our banana bread is vegan but contains wheat.
- For specific allergy questions, please speak to a barista in person or call us during opening hours. We don't want to guess.

## Other common questions
- Do you have Wi-Fi? Yes, free for customers, and laptops are welcome until 15:00 on weekdays.
- Can I book the café for a private event? Yes, on Mondays after 17:00. Email us with your date and guest count.
- Do you do wholesale? Yes, for cafés and offices. Email wholesale@marlowandpine.example.
- Do you accept gift cards from other shops? No, only our own.
- Do you take cash? Yes, and cards, Apple Pay, and Google Pay.
