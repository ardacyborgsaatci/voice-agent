# Locations and hours

## Main café: 12 Quay Street, Port Ellery
- Monday to Friday: 7:00 to 17:00
- Saturday: 8:00 to 16:00
- Sunday: 8:00 to 14:00. We're closed on Sunday afternoons.
- Free parking behind the building after 16:00. Street parking is limited during the day.

## The Annex: 88 Shore Road, Harrow Point (opened 2023)
- Our second café, 25 minutes by car from Port Ellery. It serves the full drinks and food menu, but doesn't roast beans.
- Open every day, 9:00 to 18:00.
- Has a small reading corner and a dog-friendly patio.
- Parking is free on the premises.
- Gift cards and the loyalty card work at both cafés.

## Roastery and tasting room: 3 Mill Lane, Port Ellery
- Open Thursday to Sunday, 10:00 to 15:00.
- Guided tastings take place every Saturday at 11:00. They cost 12 dollars per person and must be booked in advance.
