# About Dyzania LLP (fictional demo firm)

Everything in this folder is invented for a portfolio project. Dyzania LLP is a small law firm. Facts here are read by the voice line and by the reply drafts, so they must stay consistent with `02_help_center.md` and with the files in `firm/`.

## About the firm
Dyzania LLP is a small law firm in Harlow Point, founded in 2014 by Dana Hollis and Marcus Marr. It practices family law, personal injury, employment law, and estate planning and probate. The team is 11 people: 2 partners, 2 associate attorneys, 2 paralegals, an intake coordinator, an office manager, a billing and bookkeeping specialist, a marketing and IT specialist, and a receptionist.

## What this assistant is
- Automated assistants answer the phone line and sort the firm's email and website forms. They are not lawyers.
- They give general information about the firm. They never give legal advice, never say whether someone has a case, and never promise an outcome.
- No attorney-client relationship exists until the person and the firm sign an engagement letter.

## Contact and hours
- Office: 220 Canal Street, Suite 4, Harlow Point. Open Monday to Friday, 8:30 to 17:30.
- Phone: 555-0142. The phone line is answered by the automated voice assistant at any hour. During office hours, the receptionist can be asked for.
- General email: hello@dyzania.example.
- New case inquiries: intake@dyzania.example.
- Billing: billing@dyzania.example.
- Privacy requests: privacy@dyzania.example.
- Urgent court matters for existing clients: urgent@dyzania.example. Existing clients should also call the office during office hours.
- If anyone is in immediate danger, call 911 or the local emergency number. Do not wait for the firm.

## Booking a first consultation
- First consultations can be booked at any hour on the website booking page, by the automated voice line, or by emailing intake@dyzania.example.
- Slots are only offered when an attorney who handles that type of matter is free. The booking system checks this, so no one has to wait for the receptionist.
- A confirmation with the date, time and attorney is emailed straight away.
- Consultations are held at the office or by video, as the person prefers.

## Talking to the voice assistant
- It answers questions about practice areas, consultation fees, hours, location and what to bring.
- It can book a first consultation. It asks for a name, a phone number, an email address and the type of matter. It reads the email address back to be sure. If it cannot get the address right after two tries, it sends the person to the website booking page.
- It asks the caller not to describe confidential details by phone.
- If it does not know something, it says so and suggests emailing hello@dyzania.example.
