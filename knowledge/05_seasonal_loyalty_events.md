# Seasonal menu, loyalty card, and events

## Seasonal drinks
- Lavender latte: 5.50 dollars. Available until November 30.
- Winter spice latte: 5.50 dollars. Available December 1 to February 15. Made with cinnamon, orange peel, and clove.
- Spring honey cortado: 4.50 dollars. Available March to May.
- Summer tonic cold brew: 5.75 dollars. Available June to August. Cold brew with tonic water and a twist of lemon.

Seasonal drinks are not available in our bean subscriptions.

## Loyalty card
- Buy 9 drinks and the 10th is free. Each card is good for one free drink, which can be any drink on the menu up to 6 dollars.
- Customers can join at either café or online. There's no fee.
- Cards are linked to your phone number. If you've lost your card, give us your phone number and we'll look it up.
- Loyalty points don't carry over between seasons, and they can't be combined with other offers.

## Events
- Tuesday cupping, 8:30 to 9:30 at the main café. Founder Elena Marlow leads the session. Free, drop in, no booking needed.
- Saturday tastings at the roastery, 11:00. 12 dollars per person, book in advance.
- First Friday open mic at the Annex, 18:00 to 20:00 every first Friday of the month. Free entry, sign up at the counter on the night.
- Holiday bean sale every December. Save 20 percent on all bags from the first to the fifteenth.

## Parties and group orders
- We can prepare a coffee bar for up to 40 guests at a private event. Price depends on drinks chosen. Email events@marlowandpine.example at least two weeks ahead.
- Group orders of 10 or more drinks should be placed by phone so we can have them ready on time.
