# About Marlow & Pine (fictional test brand)

Marlow & Pine Coffee Roasters is a small specialty coffee company founded in 2016 in the harbor town of Port Ellery. We roast our own beans in small batches every week, and we sell them in our café, online, and through a few partner shops.

Our mission: make great coffee approachable, and treat the farmers who grow it fairly.

Our team is 14 people: 3 roasters, 6 baristas, 2 people in the online shop, and our founder, Elena Marlow, who still does the Tuesday morning cupping sessions.

We are a family-friendly place. Dogs are welcome on the patio.
